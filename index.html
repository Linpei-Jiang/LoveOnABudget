OUR GAMES

LOVE ON A BUDGET

Basic Info About The Game

The current game under development is a dating game in which the player must go on dates depending on their choices with you the player having to “survive” 5 days with this partner, your choices are the major contributing factor on whether you succeed or not each day. If you fail a day you get a debuff the next day. and if you fail to get the love interest you become a homeless chud.
